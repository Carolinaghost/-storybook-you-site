The styles

  • Superhero — finds a glowing cape, stops a runaway train, saves the day.

How it works

  1. Upload one photo. A clear, front-facing photo with good light works best. One photo is enough — the same face is drawn into every page.
  2. Pick a style. Superhero, Adventure, Fairy tale, Simple portrait, or “When I grow up” — firefighter, police officer or doctor.
  3. See two pages free. They're drawn from your own photo while you wait. No account and no card.
  4. Love them? Get all 15. $15 for the whole book (15 pages plus a cover). It arrives as a PDF by email in about ten minutes. Print it at home as many times as you like for your own family.

Questions

Can I choose how busy the pages are?
Yes. Simple suits ages 3–4, Standard ages 5–7, and Detailed ages 8 and up. It only changes how much is in each picture; your child still looks like your child.
How much does it cost?
$15 for a personal book and $25 for a family book with up to five people. You see two finished pages of your own photo free before you pay anything.
Is it printed and mailed?
No — it's a print-ready PDF. You download it and print it at home or at any print shop, as many times as you like for your own family.
How long does it take?
The two free pages are drawn while you wait. After you pay, the full book usually arrives by email in about ten minutes; a family book takes a little longer.
What if I don't like how it came out?
Email us within 14 days and we'll redraw your book once, free. If you're still not happy after that, we refund half of what you paid.
What happens to my photo?
It's used only to draw your book and deleted once the book is finished. Your download link works for 30 days.
